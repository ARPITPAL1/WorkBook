ABOUT NOVA CONSTRUCT

Construction Operations, Organised in One Place

NOVA CONSTRUCT provides a centralised digital workspace for real estate contractors to manage construction projects, maintain daily labour registers, monitor material purchases, track payments and outstanding bills, store legal documents, and record site progress with dated photographs.

Explore Projects

Built for Real Construction Operations

  • Project-wise daily labour attendance and wage records with gang overtime and dues settlement.
  • Material procurement, supplier invoices and payment tracking with partial settlement management.
  • Centralised financial ledger and project budget monitoring in Indian Rupees (₹ INR).
  • Secure legal and compliance document storage for municipal sanctions, RERA, and Fire NOCs.
  • Daily construction photographs and site progress reports for photographic audit evidence.
  • Weekly work plans, milestones and delay tracking across sub-structure and superstructure tasks.
  • Supervisor accounts with project-specific QR access and instant timecard check-ins.
  • Role-based permissions and financial audit trails across 7 operational disciplines.
